BRIGHTCART ELECTRONICS - QUICK SETUP AND TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
(fictional products, demonstration content)

TESSIO ANC 700 HEADPHONES - PAIRING
Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light blinks blue. Then select "Tessio ANC 700" in
the Bluetooth settings of your phone. To connect a second device (multipoint), repeat the pairing
on the second device while the first one stays connected. To reset the headphones, hold the power
button and the ANC button together for 10 seconds.

TESSIO BUDS 2 - PAIRING
Open the case lid near your phone and press the case button for 3 seconds. If only one earbud
plays, put both earbuds back in the case for 10 seconds, then take them out again.

VANTOR WATCH S - RESET AND PAIRING
Install the Vantor Fit app on your phone and pair the watch from the app, not from the Bluetooth
settings. To restart the watch, hold the side button for 15 seconds. A factory reset is in
Settings > System > Reset and erases all data on the watch.

VANTOR 2K CAMERA - WI-FI
The camera only works with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks. It cannot connect to a 5 GHz-only network.
Keep the camera within 10 metres of the router during setup.

ORBIX X5 - DUAL SIM
The SIM tray holds two nano-SIM cards. There is no memory card slot.

CALYRA BOOK 14 AND 16 PRO - KEYBOARD LANGUAGE
Press Alt + Shift to switch between French (AZERTY) and Arabic input. The first start takes
about 10 minutes; keep the laptop plugged in.

ZELKO PEN - CHARGING
Attach the pen to the magnetic side of the Zelko Tab 10 to charge it. A full charge takes about
30 minutes and lasts about 10 hours of writing.
