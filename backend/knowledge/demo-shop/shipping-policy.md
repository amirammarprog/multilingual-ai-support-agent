# Brightcart Electronics: Shipping and Delivery Policy

Brightcart Electronics is a fictional online electronics shop used for demonstrations.

## Where we deliver

We deliver to all 24 governorates of Tunisia. We do not ship outside Tunisia and we do not
offer international delivery.

## Delivery times

Orders leave our warehouse within 1 business day after the order is confirmed (cash on delivery)
or after the payment is confirmed (card or bank transfer).

| Zone | Standard delivery time |
| --- | --- |
| Grand Tunis (Tunis, Ariana, Ben Arous, Manouba) | 1 to 2 business days |
| All other governorates | 2 to 4 business days |

Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

## Delivery fees

- Standard delivery costs 7.000 TND.
- Standard delivery is free for orders of 300.000 TND or more.
- Express delivery costs 12.000 TND and is only available in Grand Tunis. Express orders confirmed
  before 13:00 are delivered the same day; later orders are delivered the next business day.
  Express delivery is not available on Sundays.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with two carriers: SwiftDrop Express and Medina Courier. When your parcel leaves the
warehouse you receive the tracking number by SMS and e-mail. You can also ask the support
assistant for the status of your order.

An estimated delivery date is shown only after the carrier has scanned the parcel. Before that
scan we cannot give a delivery date.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 3000.000 TND. Orders above 3000.000 TND must be
paid by bank card or bank transfer before shipping.

## If you are not at home

The courier calls you before delivering. After two failed delivery attempts the parcel is held at
the carrier depot for 5 days and then returned to our warehouse. You can then ask for a new
delivery; the delivery fee is charged again.

## Damaged parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, refuse it or report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
See the returns policy for the full procedure.
